# Akeia Explained

## What is Akeia?

Akeia filters negative content from your browsing experience - the content that can be detrimental to both your emotional wellbeing and your productivity.

Through digital intervention, we enable organisations to provide a safe and secure environment to their employees and empower them to be more productive and gain better mental health outcomes.

## How does Akeia work?

Akeia is able to intelligently group content through a unique application of graph technology, enabling filtering of content at a much more granular level, even down to individual HTML elements.

Content is modified or removed via an intercepting proxy using advanced AI for classification.
